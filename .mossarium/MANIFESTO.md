# Mossarium Manifesto

## Purpose

Mossarium is designed to provide a constitutional framework for software projects maintained by AI systems. It establishes guidelines and conventions that ensure consistency, maintainability, and clarity in AI-maintained codebases.

## Principles

1. **Simplicity** - Keep the system simple and easy to understand
2. **Transparency** - Make all decisions and processes visible and documented
3. **Consistency** - Enforce consistent practices across projects
4. **Maintainability** - Ensure systems can be maintained by both humans and AI
5. **Practicality** - Focus on practical solutions that solve real problems

## Philosophy

Software projects maintained by AI should follow the same principles as those maintained by humans, but with additional rigor in documentation, consistency, and adherence to established conventions.

This system is not meant to replace human judgment, but rather to provide a framework that makes AI maintenance more predictable and reliable.
