# Manifesto of the Mossarium Project

## Our Vision

The Mossarium project envisions a future where artificial intelligence plays a central role in software development, creating sustainable, efficient, and innovative solutions.

## Our Mission

To develop a framework that enables AI systems to effectively collaborate with human developers in building high-quality software products.

## Our Values

1. **Collaboration**: We believe in the power of collaboration between AI and human developers.
2. **Innovation**: We constantly strive to innovate and improve our approach to software development.
3. **Sustainability**: We focus on creating solutions that are sustainable and maintainable over time.
4. **Transparency**: We maintain transparency in our processes and decision-making.

## Our Approach

- Embrace AI as a co-developer rather than a replacement for human developers
- Maintain high standards of code quality and documentation
- Foster an environment where experimentation and learning thrive
- Build systems that can adapt and evolve with changing requirements

## Community Engagement

We welcome contributions from the community and believe in open collaboration. All participants are expected to follow our code of conduct and contribute positively to the project's growth.
